# Accessible documents: retrieval and activity prompts

Use these resources with the maintained [main guide](../Accessible_documentation_design.md) and [agent instructions](../AGENTS.md).

1. Open the [RAG reference](RAG_Reference.md) and retrieve the relevant actual source files. The index alone is not the corpus.
2. Check [source coverage](Source_Coverage.md), provenance and rights before ingesting or exporting content.
3. Choose from [eight activities and sixteen example prompts](Content_Creation_and_Production_Activities_and_Prompts.md). Replace inputs with authorised project material and keep unknowns visible.
4. Record source revision, paths and sections. Review drafts and obtain actual evidence and decisions for the requested output.
